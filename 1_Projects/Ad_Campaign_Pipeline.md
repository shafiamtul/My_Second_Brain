## 2. The 20-Second Direct-Response Video Formula

Every vertical 9:16 video ad must strictly adhere to this timing structure:

* **0:00 – 0:03 (The Scroll-Stopping Hook):** Call out a mistake, provocative question, or pattern-interrupt.
* **0:03 – 0:12 (Agitation & Fast Demo Cut):** Show rapid screen capture cuts of the actual platform / charts in action.
* **0:12 – 0:17 (The Edge / Solution):** Direct benefit statement (no lag, real-time telemetry).
* **0:17 – 0:22 (Direct CTA):** Clear action: *"Tap the link below to get instant access."*

---

## 3. Production & Editing Guidelines

1. **Voiceover:**
   * Style: Fast-Paced Financial / Commercial Anchor (~160 words per minute).
   * Tone: Authoritative, punchy downward inflection on numbers and tickers.
2. **Video Editing (CapCut / Premiere):**
   * Aspect Ratio: `9:16` (1080 × 1920).
   * Cut Frequency: Switch zoom, pan, or b-roll every `1.0 to 1.5 seconds`.
   * Captions: Dynamic 1–2 word auto-captions with high-contrast yellow or bright green highlighting.
   * Background Music: Low-volume phonk, tech-house, or dark industrial synth (ducked under voice).
