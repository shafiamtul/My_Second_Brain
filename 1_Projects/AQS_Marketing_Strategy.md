# Active Project: AQS Marketing Strategy & Execution

## 1. Executive Overview
Atlas Quant Systems (AQS) provides quantitative market research, real-time CME futures order flow telemetry, options dealer gamma (GEX) calculations, and proprietary NinjaTrader 8 trading suites.

This document serves as the shared single source of truth for **Tariq** and **Shafia** to co-manage, execute, and scale marketing across organic and paid acquisition channels.
